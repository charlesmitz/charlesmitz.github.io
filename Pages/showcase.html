RMIT Games Showcase 2021

Online exhibition showcasing capstone games from the 2021 graduates of the RMIT Bachelor of Design (Games). Co-designed with RMIT Games alumni Tanya Wong.



Online space hosted on Glitch.me, made using existing LikeLike Online framework. Responsibilities were shared across the project and included production, design, and illustration.
