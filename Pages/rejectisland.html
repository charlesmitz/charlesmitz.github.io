Reject Island

Reject Island is a narrative driven game set on the titual island where forgotten and unloved characters from folklore and mythology are sent to live out their final days. Players explore the island as corporate intern Tuesday Taylor, collecting data and getting to know the colourful inhabitants.



Graduate project, 'Reject Island' is a vertical slice made over the course of 13 weeks in a 5 person team.
