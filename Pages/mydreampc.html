My Dream PC

Designed as a loving tribute to the thriving custom PC community, My Dream PC invites players to share the joy of customising in a safe, cosy digital world.



My Dream PC celebrates the skills of the community and highlights that decorating and customising are as integral to building as choosing the right parts.



Originally made in two months, the demo debuted at the Feminine Play exhibition as a part of Melbourne International Games Week 2024. It is currently on display at ACMI in the free exhibition The Story of The Moving Image, until mid 2026.
