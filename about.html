About

I'm a multi-disciplinary designer with a background in game design, currently living and working in Melbourne, Australia. This site contains a portfolio of my work across creative practices, with a focus on digital and visual design.

I'm currently working as an Interface Designer and Assoc. Lecturer at RMIT. I'm interested in creating functional, beautiful designs that support the worlds they're made for.

When I'm not obsessing over making things look good, you can find me doing laps at the roller rink or plotting my next big adventure.
