About Me

I am a designer with a background in Games Design, currently living and working in Melbourne, Australia.
This site contains a portfolio of my work across creative practices, with a focus on digital and visual design.

I'm interested in creating worlds that don't look like our own, with an eye for bold colours and aesthetics.

When I’m not obsessing over making things look good, you can find me doing laps at the roller rink or plotting my next big adventure.
